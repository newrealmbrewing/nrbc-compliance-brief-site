Top Story

Illinois agrees to pause hemp THC enforcement for about 250 businesses until Nov. 19

ILLINOIS • The Marijuana Herald • Nov 9

The state agreed in federal court to hold off enforcing its new hemp-derived THC law against the roughly 250 member companies of the Illinois Healthy Alternatives Association and other plaintiffs in Illinois Healthy Alternatives Association v. Raoul (N.D. Ill., filed Nov. 5), while U.S. District Judge Martha M. Pacold prepares to hear arguments Nov. 19 on whether to block the law during the case. The law classifies consumable hemp products above 0.4 mg of total THC per container as marijuana that can be sold only at licensed dispensaries; the pause does not suspend it statewide, and the Illinois Department of Revenue confirmed enforcement against everyone else begins Nov. 12. CBS Chicago reported Glenn McElfresh of the Hemp Beverage Alliance said major retailers' shelves are already "just bone dry."

Why it matters: Illinois retailers and distributors outside the lawsuit must still pull hemp THC beverages above 0.4 mg from shelves by this Thursday, Nov. 12 — the Nov. 19 hearing decides whether relief extends any further.

Federal

Congressional researchers say FDA is nine months late on required hemp cannabinoid lists and "container" guidance

FEDERAL • Marijuana Moment • Nov 9

A Congressional Research Service report says FDA missed its Feb. 10 statutory deadline to publish lists of naturally occurring cannabinoids, THC-class cannabinoids, and similar-effect cannabinoids, along with guidance defining the "container" that anchors the new 0.4 mg total THC federal limit. Jim Higdon of hemp producer Cornbread Hemp said it "is difficult to comply with the law while key definitions are still in question."

Why it matters: the pending "container" definition determines which hemp beverages can remain on shelves when the federal hemp definition change takes effect Dec. 11.

DEA proposes 2027 marijuana production quotas, with separate Schedule III limits to follow

FEDERAL • The Marijuana Herald • Nov 9

DEA's proposed 2027 aggregate production quotas, scheduled for Federal Register publication Nov. 10, keep Schedule I marijuana at 6,675 kilograms and marijuana extract at 1,000 kilograms — unchanged from 2026 — and exclude marijuana moved to Schedule III. The agency said it is registering Schedule III manufacturers and distributors and will set separate Schedule III quotas in a later notice, with the proposal open to public comment before a final order.

Around the States

Illinois Department of Revenue: local cannabis retail tax rates change Jan. 1

ILLINOIS • Illinois Dept. of Revenue • Nov 6

Bulletin FY 2027-06 announces municipal and county cannabis retailers' occupation tax rate changes effective Jan. 1, 2027, in jurisdictions that imposed or changed local rates by ordinance; the statewide 6.25% retailers' occupation tax and local general-merchandise taxes continue to apply on top. The full bulletin lists each affected jurisdiction and its new rate.

Why it matters: dispensary retailers in the listed jurisdictions need updated point-of-sale tax rates loaded before Jan. 1 to collect correctly.

On the Radar

Thursday, Nov. 12 — The federal ban on synthetic cannabinoids not naturally produced by the cannabis plant takes effect, and Illinois's 0.4 mg hemp THC law becomes enforceable against businesses not covered by the lawsuit pause.

Thursday, Nov. 19 — Judge Pacold (N.D. Ill.) hears arguments on a preliminary injunction against Illinois's hemp THC law; the agreed enforcement pause for plaintiff businesses runs through that date.

Friday, Dec. 11 — The new federal hemp definition (0.4 mg total THC per container) takes effect for most products after Congress's one-month delay, with FDA's statutorily required definitions still unpublished.
