Top Story

Texas court takes up pausing THC enforcement as hemp businesses sue state health agency

TEXAS • CBS Austin • Aug 28

A Travis County judge was set to hear arguments Friday afternoon on whether to temporarily pause enforcement of the Texas Department of State Health Services standards in effect since July 31, which treat delta-8, delta-10, THCP, THCA flower and synthetic THC forms as Schedule I substances and permit only naturally occurring trace amounts alongside delta-9 hemp products at or under 0.3%. Hemp businesses, which filed the suit August 19, argue the state is applying an undefined “trace amount” standard without formal rulemaking; Mark Bordas of the Texas Hemp Business Council said continued enforcement puts an “11 billion dollar-a-year industry” at risk. Any order from the hearing would be temporary, not a final ruling.

Why it matters: The outcome sets the near-term shelf status for hemp THC products in one of the largest U.S. retail markets — retailers and distributors carrying delta-8 or THCA-adjacent inventory in Texas face immediate stocking decisions either way.

Federal

D.C. Circuit sets briefing schedule in challenge to Medicare hemp coverage program

FEDERAL • Marijuana Moment • Aug 27

The appeals court scheduled opening briefs for October 5 and final briefs for December 16 in the appeal brought by a coalition led by Smart Approaches to Marijuana and MMJ International Holdings, after Judge Trevor McFadden dismissed the case in May for lack of standing. The CMS program at issue covers up to $500 a year in hemp-derived products — capped at 0.3% delta-9 THC and 3 milligrams total THC per serving — for eligible seniors.

Why it matters: The case keeps a federal hemp product program in litigation into 2027, and the standing question will shape who can challenge federal hemp rules — including the THC-per-serving definitions that beverage makers watch closely.

Around the States

Virginia hemp businesses press lawmakers for a hearing on the new 2 mg THC cap

VIRGINIA • Marijuana Moment • Aug 27

The Cannabis Small Business Association, on behalf of seven hemp companies, asked the legislature's joint cannabis oversight commission to hold a hearing on the law in effect since August 15, which removed the 25:1 CBD-to-THC ratio exception and caps hemp products at 2 milligrams of total THC per package. Commission chair Sen. Lashrecse Aird (D) said September meetings will focus on “health and safety, public health awareness and enforcement”; a federal judge earlier declined to block the law, and the commission has not said whether it will grant the hearing.

Why it matters: The 2 mg cap keeps most hemp THC beverages off Virginia shelves roughly 11 months before adult-use retail opens July 1, 2027 — near-term market access there now rides on whether lawmakers revisit the limit.

California opens farmers'-market wine sales to non-estate small producers

CALIFORNIA • KSBY • Aug 27

Gov. Gavin Newsom signed Sen. John Laird's SB 917, which lets small winemakers sell and pour at certified farmers' markets without the prior requirement that all grapes be estate-grown, effective January 1, 2027. Family Winemakers of California and the California Association of Winegrape Growers sponsored the bill.

Florida hemp sellers urge Congress to revisit 0.4 mg drink limit before November

FLORIDA • WEAR News • Aug 28

Pensacola-area hemp sellers said the federal redefinition taking effect November 12 — capping hemp drinks and gummies at 0.4 milligrams of total THC per container, versus the 5–10 milligrams common today — would remove most current products, and urged Congress to act before the deadline. Staff at Pensacola's Perfect Plain Brewery described THC beverages as a significant share of sales this summer.

Why it matters: Taprooms and retailers carrying hemp beverages face fall inventory decisions while the Senate-passed delay to December 11 awaits House action.

On the Radar

Sept. 8 — Canada's federal counter-tariffs on more than 700 U.S. goods take effect; provincial liquor measures, including Saskatchewan's 50% retail levy on U.S. alcohol, begin the same day.

Oct. 5 — Opening appellate briefs due in the D.C. Circuit challenge to the Medicare hemp coverage program.

Nov. 12 — Federal hemp redefinition (0.4 mg total THC per container) takes effect unless the Senate-passed delay to Dec. 11 clears the House; hemp beverage regulation bills remain pending in both chambers.
