Top Story

Oregon hemp suppliers wind down operations ahead of December 11 federal THC limits

OREGON / FEDERAL • Marijuana Moment • Oct 3

Oregon hemp retailers and suppliers say the federal hemp redefinition enacted last November is forcing closures ahead of its effective date, when finished hemp products containing more than 0.4 milligrams of total THC per container lose their federal hemp status. Oregon CBD — whose co-owner Seth Crawford says supplies the large majority of U.S. hemp seed production — plans to close by the end of the month; Crawford said the law "essentially ends the hemp industry." Congress moved the effective date from November 12 to December 11, 2026 in the September continuing resolution, per a Dykema client alert.

Why it matters: Distributors and retailers carrying hemp-THC beverages face a hard federal cutoff on December 11, and supplier exits this fall may disrupt sourcing and inventory well before that date — worth confirming supply commitments and sell-through plans for affected SKUs now.

Federal

Four senators ask DOJ to investigate Chinese criminal networks in cannabis and hemp markets

FEDERAL • Cannabis Business Times • Oct 1

Sens. Tom Cotton (R-AR), Susan Collins (R-ME), Pete Ricketts (R-NE) and Ted Budd (R-NC) wrote to Attorney General Todd Blanche requesting an investigation into Chinese transnational criminal organizations' financing, land use and chemical imports in U.S. cannabis and hemp markets, writing that criminal networks are "exploiting vulnerabilities in federal law." The letter signals continued federal enforcement attention on cannabis and hemp supply chains ahead of the December transition.

Around the States

Virginia regulators survey medical cannabis patients ahead of adult-use launch

VIRGINIA • Marijuana Moment • Oct 2

The Virginia Cannabis Control Authority opened a patient survey covering pricing, access and product availability — including how patients expect their purchasing to change when adult-use retail launches in July 2027. Responses are due October 16. The survey is part of the CCA's preparation for the medical-to-adult-use transition in a market where license applications open February 1, 2027.

North Carolina advisory panel weighs public-consumption limits for recommendations to governor

NORTH CAROLINA • Marijuana Moment • Oct 3

North Carolina's Advisory Council on Cannabis reviewed draft recommendations that would prohibit THC consumption in public places and moving vehicles, decline to authorize consumption lounges, and rely on civil fines for enforcement; law-enforcement members said municipal agencies lack statutory authority for civil enforcement. Subcommittees meet through October and November, with final recommendations due to Gov. Josh Stein (D) by December 31.

Pennsylvania LCB adds production reporting to manufacturer license renewals

PENNSYLVANIA • National Law Review (Norris McLaughlin) • Sep 28

License-structure note for PA partners: the Pennsylvania Liquor Control Board's renewal applications now require manufacturers to report total gallons produced on the licensed premises in the prior calendar year, excluding contract production. The question applies to the brewery, brewpub, distillery, limited distillery, winery and limited winery license classes — a check against class production limits (limited distilleries, for example, are capped at 100,000 gallons annually; breweries have a 200-gallon minimum and no cap). No fee amounts change under this update.

Why it matters: Pennsylvania licensees should have 2025 production records ready at renewal; reporting against class limits can affect whether an operation keeps its current license class.

On the Radar

October 12 — 60-day WARN Act notice deadline for hemp operators with 100 or more employees planning closures or layoffs tied to the federal hemp change, per MJBizDaily.

December 11 — the federal hemp redefinition takes effect: finished hemp products over 0.4 mg total THC per container lose federal hemp status. The date moved from November 12 under the September continuing resolution.

Late October — Virginia's Cannabis Control Authority preliminary-feedback window on draft adult-use retail rules; final rules are expected in December and license applications open February 1, 2027.
