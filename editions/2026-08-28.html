Top Story

Saskatchewan keeps U.S. alcohol on shelves but adds a 50% retaliatory levy Sept. 8

CANADA • Global News • Aug 26

Premier Scott Moe announced Saskatchewan will impose a 50% levy on American alcoholic beverages effective September 8 — the same day Canada's federal counter-tariffs on more than 700 U.S. goods take effect — while keeping the products on store shelves, unlike Manitoba, which has pulled them entirely. Moe said U.S. alcohol sales in the province already fell about 40% over the past fiscal year, and said consumer choice "sends a much stronger message than any government-ordered ban." Alberta remains the only province without such measures.

Why it matters: Another provincial market tightens terms for U.S. beverage exports ahead of the Sept. 8 counter-tariff date — a 50% levy at retail affects pricing, listings, and forecast volumes for any U.S. brewery or distributor with Canadian placements.

Federal

CDC proposes marijuana-use questions for the 2028 National Health Interview Survey

FEDERAL • Marijuana Moment • Aug 26

The CDC proposed adding questions asking how often respondents use marijuana and whether use is for medical reasons or "for fun or to get high." Public comments run into mid-October.

Why it matters: Federal survey data separating medical from recreational use would feed directly into future scheduling, labeling, and beverage-policy debates that shape the THC drinks category.

Around the States

Pennsylvania senator presses hemp regulation bill as federal ban date nears

PENNSYLVANIA • Erie News Now • Aug 25

Sen. Dan Laughlin (R) said his bill would set testing, labeling, and age-21 rules for hemp-derived products, including beverages, rather than banning them: it "will simply regulate it and give clear guidelines on what's legal to sell." Erie County District Attorney Elizabeth Hirz cited enforcement gaps under current law, saying officials are told products are "Farm Bill compliant, so there's nothing you can do."

Why it matters: A regulate-not-ban framework in Pennsylvania would keep a large retail market open to compliant hemp beverages — with new testing and labeling obligations for producers selling there.

Michigan regulator suspends Lansing hemp processor-handler license

MICHIGAN • Michigan Cannabis Regulatory Agency • Aug 25

The CRA summarily suspended Loud Labs of Michigan's hemp processor-handler license and filed to revoke it, alleging products on site exceeded the 0.3% delta-9 THC limit during a March inspection and May audit. The agency noted the allegations remain unproven pending the administrative process.

Iowa governor's race splits on hemp THC beverage sales

IOWA • Marijuana Moment • Aug 26

Democratic nominee Rob Sand said he would reverse the state's 2024 ban on consumable hemp sales and allow regulated hemp and THC beverage products, arguing cannabis should be regulated "the way we treat alcohol." Republican nominee Zach Lahn opposes legalization, per available reporting.

Why it matters: November's result could reopen — or keep closed — Iowa's retail channel for hemp THC beverages.

Missouri draws nearly 900 applicants for 77 cannabis microbusiness licenses

MISSOURI • MJBizDaily • Aug 27

The Missouri Lottery will draw winners September 9 in the state's final microbusiness round — at least 29 wholesale and 34 retail licenses — after regulators tightened ownership-review rules in May following earlier revocations.

On the Radar

Sept. 8 — Canada's federal counter-tariffs on more than 700 U.S. goods take effect; provincial liquor measures, including Saskatchewan's 50% levy, begin the same day.

Sept. 30 — Ohio regulators hold a hearing on hemp rule changes.

Nov. 12 — Federal hemp redefinition (0.4 mg total THC per container) takes effect unless the Senate-passed delay to Dec. 11 clears the House; hemp beverage regulation bills remain pending in both chambers.
