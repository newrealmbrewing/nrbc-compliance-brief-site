TOP STORY

Justice Department and challengers propose briefing timeline in marijuana rescheduling appeal

FEDERAL • Marijuana Moment • Oct 9

The federal government and the parties challenging the administration's move of state-licensed medical marijuana products to Schedule III filed a joint proposed briefing schedule Friday in the U.S. Court of Appeals for the D.C. Circuit, which is weighing three consolidated lawsuits brought by Smart Approaches to Marijuana and the National Drug and Alcohol Screening Association, a coalition that includes a cannabis biopharmaceutical company, and the attorneys general of Indiana and Nebraska. Under the proposal, the challengers' consolidated opening brief is due November 24, the government's response January 13, and final briefs February 19 — a pace the filing says would allow oral argument before the court's summer recess. The court earlier declined to pause the rescheduling process while the litigation continues.

Why it matters: The schedule signals no court-ordered disruption to Schedule III-based programs — including the DEA supply-chain registrations now open — before spring 2027, so partners can plan 2027 assortments on current rules while watching the November 24 filing.

FEDERAL

Rep. Mast calls for ‘across-the-board’ review of federal drug scheduling

FEDERAL • Marijuana Moment • Oct 9

Rep. Brian Mast (R-FL), a co-chair of the Congressional Cannabis Caucus, said the administration's cannabis rescheduling should expand into an examination of scheduling for other controlled substances, naming cannabis, hemp and psilocybin among categories to review. A recent Government Accountability Office report found the DEA and FDA lack formal policies governing how staff evaluate substances for scheduling recommendations. The comments signal congressional interest only; no rule change is proposed.

AROUND THE STATES

Sierra Nevada terminates Coors Distributing Co., shifting Denver-area rights to Reyes

COLORADO • Brewbound • Oct 7

Sierra Nevada Brewing Co. is ending its agreement with Coors Distributing Co. and will move its Denver-area distribution to Reyes, Brewbound reported.

Why it matters: Brand realignments of this scale reshuffle route-to-market economics for neighboring wholesalers, and affected retail accounts should expect transition notices on ordering and delivery as the move is completed.

Diageo files WARN notice for 174 layoffs in Guinness Open Gate Brewery closure

MARYLAND • Brewbound • Oct 8

Diageo's closure of the Guinness Open Gate Brewery in Halethorpe, Maryland will result in 174 permanent layoffs effective November 1, according to a state WARN-notice filing reported by Brewbound.

Colorado regulators issue guidance on inactive ingredients in cannabis vape devices

COLORADO • Marijuana Moment • Oct 9

Colorado cannabis regulators published guidance addressing inactive ingredients added to marijuana vaporizer devices, per Marijuana Moment's October 9 roundup. Licensed partners carrying vape SKUs in Colorado should check formulations against the new guidance.

ON THE RADAR

Nov. 12 — Missouri's intoxicating-hemp law takes effect: hemp THC products move under the state's licensed cannabis system and online sales end, while hemp beverages may stay on general-retail shelves until the federal cutoff, with sales restricted to buyers 21 and over in the interim.

Dec. 11 — The federal restrictions on hemp-derived THC products enacted in the November 2025 appropriations law take effect, after a one-month delay signed by President Trump — the date that would end most intoxicating hemp beverage sales nationwide absent further congressional action.

Cert watch — A petition asking the U.S. Supreme Court to resolve a circuit split on whether state residency requirements for cannabis licenses violate the dormant Commerce Clause was docketed September 15; decisions on review typically take two to five months.
