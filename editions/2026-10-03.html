Top Story

FTC Finalizes Pricing-Discrimination Settlement With Southern Glazer's

FEDERAL • Federal Trade Commission • Oct 2

The FTC announced a settlement resolving its 2024 Robinson-Patman Act suit against Southern Glazer's Wine & Spirits, the nation's largest alcohol distributor. Under the six-year stipulated order, approved 2–0, Southern may not charge small, independent retailers significantly more than large chains for identical products; qualifying violations require Southern to pay affected retailers 1.5 times the price differential, with double damages available through FTC enforcement, and an independent monitor will oversee compliance. Bureau of Competition Director Daniel Guarnera said the settlement "marks a significant milestone" in Robinson-Patman enforcement.

Why it matters: Independent retailers buying from Southern gain an enforceable right to pricing parity with chains on identical SKUs, and distributors across the industry now have a concrete template for what Robinson-Patman compliance looks like — discount and rebate programs are worth re-reviewing against it.

Around the States

Two New Adult-Use Legalization Measures Filed for Florida's 2028 Ballot

FLORIDA • Marijuana Moment • Oct 2

Two campaigns filed 2028 constitutional amendments to legalize marijuana for adults 21 and over. The "Floridians for Marijuana" measure would let existing medical dispensaries sell recreationally within six months of approval and open licensing to new businesses without vertical-integration requirements; the "Let Florida Vote IV" measure would create a licensing authority beyond the Department of Health and cap license fees at the cost of administration. Each needs 880,062 valid signatures to qualify, and passage requires 60 percent of the vote.

Why it matters: Both measures would end Florida's vertically integrated license structure — a potential opening for independent distribution and retail entrants in the third-largest state market, with the fee-cap provision bearing directly on future license costs.

New York Adult-Use Sales Pass $4.1 Billion; Retail Network Reaches 918 Stores

NEW YORK • Marijuana Moment • Oct 2

The state Office of Cannabis Management announced cumulative recreational sales of $4.1 billion since the market opened in late 2022, including $512.6 million in the third quarter of 2026. New York now counts 918 licensed retail locations and 1,981 licensed cannabis businesses overall, with 56 percent of recreational licenses held by social and economic equity applicants.

Virginia Regulator Surveys Medical Patients Ahead of Adult-Use Transition

VIRGINIA • Marijuana Moment • Oct 2

The Virginia Cannabis Control Authority opened a satisfaction survey of the state's roughly 114,000 registered medical cannabis patients, open through October 16, asking about pricing, access and product preferences — and whether patients expect to shift purchases to adult-use retail stores once recreational sales launch. Acting head Jamie Patten said the agency wants input from "the people who know Virginia's medical cannabis program firsthand" as it prepares the market transition.

Why it matters: The survey is an early read on how much medical volume may migrate to Virginia's adult-use retail channel, which is on track for final rules in December and license applications opening February 1, 2027.

On the Radar

December 11, 2026 — The federal ban on intoxicating hemp products takes effect, after the September continuing resolution moved the date from November 12. Congress continues to weigh regulatory frameworks for low-dose THC beverages and full-spectrum CBD during the added time.

November 3, 2026 — Massachusetts votes on Question 8, which would repeal regulated commercial cannabis sales and home cultivation while keeping possession and the medical program legal. Campaign advertising is underway on both sides.

Late October — Virginia's Cannabis Control Authority preliminary-feedback window on its draft adult-use retail regulations approaches; final rules are expected in December 2026, with license applications opening February 1, 2027 and retail sales targeted for July 1, 2027.
