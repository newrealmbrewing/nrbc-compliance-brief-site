Top Story

California Adds Serving-Size Labeling Rules for THC Beverages and Edibles

CALIFORNIA • Marijuana Moment • Oct 1

Gov. Gavin Newsom (D) signed AB 2532, which requires cannabis edibles and beverages to carry the national Poison Help line number and sets new rules for multi-serving THC drinks: labels must state the number of servings and the serving size in fluid ounces, transparent containers must carry conspicuous lines marking individual servings, and retailers must supply a measuring device. Marketing may not present a multi-serving drink as single-serve or encourage consuming multiple servings at once. The requirements take effect July 1, 2027.

Why it matters: Any multi-serving THC beverage sold in California will need redesigned labels, container markings and retail measuring devices by July 1, 2027 — packaging and SKU planning for 2027 should account for it now.

Federal

Four Senators Ask DOJ to Investigate Chinese Role in U.S. Marijuana and Hemp Markets

FEDERAL • Marijuana Moment • Oct 1

Sens. Tom Cotton (R-AR), Ted Budd (R-NC), Pete Ricketts (R-NE) and Susan Collins (R-ME) sent a letter Wednesday to Attorney General Todd Blanche asking the Justice Department to investigate Chinese criminal organizations' involvement in U.S. marijuana and hemp markets, writing that such groups are "exploiting vulnerabilities in federal law" through illegal grow operations and imported pesticides.

Why it matters: A DOJ inquiry would add federal enforcement attention to hemp supply chains — documentation of sourcing and ingredient provenance becomes more valuable for anyone handling hemp-derived products.

Around the States

Georgia Approves Mail Delivery of Medical Cannabis to Registered Patients

GEORGIA • Marijuana Moment • Oct 1

The Georgia Access to Medical Cannabis Commission approved a rule allowing licensed producers to ship low-THC oil products to registered patients by USPS and other carriers, using certified mail with tracking and a signature restricted to the patient, caregiver or healthcare-facility employee. The carriers have not confirmed they will participate.

Martignetti Closes RNDC Control-State Acquisition, Adding 17 Markets

MULTI-STATE • Brewbound • Oct 1

Martignetti Companies completed its acquisition of Republic National Distributing Company's control-state distribution business on October 1, extending its operations into 17 state markets where alcohol is sold through state-run systems.

Why it matters: Suppliers and retail partners in control states may see wholesaler account and service transitions as the RNDC book moves to Martignetti — worth confirming rep coverage and ordering continuity in affected markets.

On the Radar

December 11, 2026 — The federal ban on intoxicating hemp products takes effect, after the September continuing resolution moved the date from November 12. Congress is using the added month to weigh regulatory frameworks for low-dose THC beverages and full-spectrum CBD.

November 3, 2026 — Massachusetts votes on Question 8, which would repeal regulated commercial cannabis sales and home cultivation while keeping possession and the medical program legal. Opposition ads began running this week.

Late October — Virginia's Cannabis Control Authority preliminary-feedback window on its draft adult-use retail regulations approaches; final rules are expected in December 2026, with license applications opening February 1, 2027.
