Top Story

Seventh Circuit hears challenge to Illinois ban on out-of-state wine shipments

ILLINOIS • Bloomberg Law • Sep 17

A three-judge panel of the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Seventh Circuit heard oral argument Thursday over Illinois’s prohibition on direct-to-consumer wine shipments from out-of-state sellers. Attorney James Tanford, arguing for the challengers, said the 21st Amendment’s grant of state authority over alcohol does not justify a complete ban on interstate shipments; Illinois defended the restriction as protecting residents’ health and safety by keeping alcohol subject to in-person inspection.

Why it matters: A ruling against the state could open one of the largest U.S. markets to out-of-state direct shipping and would signal how far courts will let states restrict cross-border alcohol sales — a decision worth watching for every distributor and retailer operating near state lines.

Federal

Bipartisan bill would shield insurers that cover state-licensed cannabis businesses

FEDERAL • Marijuana Moment • Sep 17

Reps. Nydia Velázquez (D-NY) and Warren Davidson (R-OH) introduced the Clarifying Law Around Insurance of Marijuana (CLAIM) Act, H.R. 10471, which would bar federal agencies from penalizing insurers, brokers and agents for serving state-licensed cannabis businesses and would prohibit terminating policies based solely on a cannabis connection. Velázquez said that “because of the conflict between federal and state law, insurers are still hesitant to write policies.”

Why it matters: Retailers and distributors that handle THC products often face limited or costly coverage; a federal safe harbor would widen the pool of carriers willing to write those policies.

Around the States

Colorado expands residual-solvent testing; issues two contamination advisories

COLORADO • Colorado Marijuana Enforcement Division • Sep 11–15

The Marijuana Enforcement Division’s Industry Bulletin 26:09 implements expanded residual-solvent testing requirements for regulated products. Separately, MED and the state health department issued health-and-safety advisories on Sep 10 (Navajo Management LLC) and Sep 15 (CC Brands LLC, dba Stash House CO) for contaminated vaporizer products.

Why it matters: Retail partners in Colorado should pull affected batches; suppliers should expect added testing cost and turnaround time as the expanded panel takes hold.

Alaska files proposed rule changes on THC limits for cannabis edibles

ALASKA • Marijuana Moment • Sep 17

Alaska regulators filed proposed rule changes addressing THC limits for cannabis edibles, per Marijuana Moment’s state roundup. Comment and adoption timelines will follow the state’s standard rulemaking process.

On the Radar

Sep 29 — The executive order barring imports of Canadian beer, wine and spirits takes effect; product entered for consumption before that date remains subject to the existing 50% duty instead (Shanken News Daily). Retailers carrying Canadian brands should settle inventory and pricing plans now.

Dec 11 — The federal ban on consumable hemp products above 0.4 mg total THC per container takes effect, after Congress moved the date back from Nov 12 in this month’s continuing resolution; synthetic cannabinoids not naturally producible from the plant are recriminalized on the original Nov 12 date (Marijuana Moment).
