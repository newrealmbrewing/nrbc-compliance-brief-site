Good morning — here's the regulatory picture for alcohol and hemp/THC beverages, readable in about three minutes. Every headline links to the original source.

A quiet weekend for new agency actions — today's edition runs lighter and draws on the past 72 hours, with the standing federal timeline items in On the Radar.

Top Story

Denver's Coors Distributing says it plans to fight Constellation and Mark Anthony terminations

COLORADO • Brewbound • Sep 25

Constellation Brands notified Denver-based Coors Distributing Company (CDC), a wholly owned Molson Coors subsidiary, on September 11 that it intends to terminate their distribution relationship within 30 days, and Mark Anthony Brands — maker of White Claw — is moving its portfolio to RBG Spirits and Wine of Colorado, a Reyes Beverage Group subsidiary, in the same market. Brewbound reported Friday that CDC plans to fight both terminations rather than let the brands exit quietly.

Why it matters: How Colorado's beer-franchise and distribution-agreement protections apply to two simultaneous large-supplier exits will be watched by wholesalers in every state, and Denver-area retail accounts should plan for possible supplier service transitions — and confirm ordering and delivery contacts — as soon as October.

Federal

Sen. Scott files amendment barring college-athlete NIL deals that promote alcohol or cannabis

FEDERAL • Marijuana Moment • Sep 25

Sen. Tim Scott (R-SC) filed an amendment to the pending college sports bill that would prohibit student-athlete name, image and likeness agreements promoting alcoholic beverages, marijuana, gambling, and tobacco or nicotine products. If the amendment is adopted, beverage brands and their retail partners using college-athlete endorsements would need to unwind or restructure those campaigns.

Around the States

Massachusetts governor proposes veteran medical cannabis cards on VA documentation, tax-free purchases

MASSACHUSETTS • Marijuana Moment • Sep 24

Gov. Maura Healey (D) filed a supplemental budget bill that would let military veterans obtain a state medical cannabis card using Veterans Administration documentation of a qualifying condition, without a separate physician's recommendation. Medical purchases are exempt from the state's sales, excise and municipal cannabis taxes and carry higher purchase and potency limits than the adult-use market.

Why it matters: If enacted, a share of Massachusetts adult-use volume would shift to the untaxed medical channel — a margin and pricing consideration for retailers and distributors serving both markets there.

California enacts Braun's Law and a package of repeat-DUI enforcement bills

CALIFORNIA • Governor's Office • Sep 25

Gov. Gavin Newsom signed SB 907 ("Braun's Law"), which requires formal impaired-driving warnings when DUI charges are reduced, along with AB 1546 (stronger penalties for repeat DUI offenders), AB 1685 (more DMV points for intoxicated vehicular manslaughter) and AB 1687 (license revocations up to six years for drivers with four or more qualifying convictions in 10 years). The package targets drivers rather than licensees; no new obligations fall on alcohol sellers or servers.

On the Radar

Tuesday, September 29 — The presidential order excluding most Canadian packaged wine, spirits and beer from U.S. importation takes effect at 12:01 a.m. ET; product imported earlier but not yet entered for consumption stays subject to the existing 50% duty (Shanken News Daily). In-transit Canadian SKUs must be entered before the deadline.

Late October — The Virginia Cannabis Control Authority's preliminary-feedback window on its draft adult-use retail regulations is expected; final rules are due in December, license applications open February 1, 2027, and retail sales launch July 1, 2027 (Virginia CCA).

November 12 / December 11 — Missouri's state restrictions on hemp THC products take effect November 12, and the federal hemp redefinition limiting legal products to 0.4 mg total THC per container takes effect December 11 (Marijuana Moment).
