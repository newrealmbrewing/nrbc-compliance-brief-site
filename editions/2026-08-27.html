Top Story

DEA publishes full rescheduling hearing transcript as Schedule III decision nears

FEDERAL • MJBizDaily • Aug 26

The Drug Enforcement Administration released the complete 2,533-page transcript of its 11-day cannabis rescheduling hearing, which concluded July 15. In the record, DEA attorney James Schwartz argued that marijuana "can no longer remain in Schedule 1" because it has a currently accepted medical use, citing more than 30,000 practitioners treating over six million patients across 43 jurisdictions. Chief Administrative Law Judge Derek Julius will now issue a formal recommendation; the final decision rests with DEA Administrator Terrance Cole.

Why it matters: A move to Schedule III would change federal tax treatment for cannabis businesses and could accelerate investment in THC products that increasingly compete with beer at retail — and the timing now hinges on the judge's recommendation.

Federal

Supreme Court expected to weigh federal home-distilling ban this fall

U.S. SUPREME COURT • SCOTUSblog • Aug 26

Two petitions filed this summer ask the justices to resolve a circuit split over the 158-year-old federal ban on at-home distilling of spirits, after the Sixth Circuit upheld the ban and the Fifth Circuit struck it down. The court is likely to consider both petitions at conferences in October or November.

Why it matters: The case tests how far federal taxing power reaches into alcohol production — a ruling either way could redraw the boundary between federal and state authority over who may produce spirits, and where.

Around the States

New Mexico credit union to close its cannabis banking arm Nov. 1

NEW MEXICO • MJBizDaily • Aug 25

Albuquerque-based U.S. Eagle Federal Credit Union said it will shut down Aery Group, the cannabis banking division it launched in 2019, effective November 1; CEO Michael Moore cited intensifying competition, with a consultant quoted in the report counting at least 19 other providers now serving New Mexico cannabis businesses.

Why it matters: Businesses banking through Aery — including beverage makers with cannabis or hemp lines — need replacement accounts in place before the cutoff to keep deposits, payments, and payroll moving.

Illinois adds two qualifying conditions to its medical cannabis program

ILLINOIS • Marijuana Moment • Aug 25

The Illinois Department of Public Health added sickle cell disease and polyendocrine metabolic ovarian syndrome with chronic pain as qualifying conditions, bringing the state's medical cannabis program to 58 conditions following Medical Cannabis Advisory Board review.

On the Radar

Sept. 8: Canada's announced retaliatory tariffs on U.S. goods, including alcohol, are set to take effect — watch for updates affecting U.S. beer and spirits exports and provincial restocking commitments.

Sept. 30: FDA's stated target for initial hemp cannabinoid guidance; a Congressional Research Service report covered last week says the agency's cannabinoid lists and container guidance are already months overdue.

Nov. 12: Federal hemp THC caps take effect under last year's spending law unless Congress acts; House members remain divided on a delay, with hemp beverage businesses planning around the current date.
