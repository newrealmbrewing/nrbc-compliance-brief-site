Top Story

Craft brewers press Ohio Senate to overhaul the state's 1974 beer franchise law

OHIO • The Statehouse News Bureau • Sep 16

Senate Bill 23, reintroduced in January by Sen. Andrew Brenner (R-Delaware), would rewrite the 50-year-old law governing agreements between brewers and their wholesalers, excluding many smaller breweries from the statute's strict franchise requirements and easing the automatic-renewal provisions that currently bind them to a distributor. The Ohio Craft Brewers Association's Mary MacDonald said hundreds of small Ohio breweries are disadvantaged against much larger distributors, and founders of three closed breweries testified in favor; Wholesale Beer and Wine Association of Ohio counsel Jacob Evans countered that the bill would let brewers walk away from a contract without consequence. The bill has stalled in committee, and the renewed testimony — picked up by Brewbound on Friday — is an effort to restart it.

Why it matters: If SB 23 advances, Ohio brewers below the exemption threshold could change wholesalers without proving cause, and distributors would need to re-underwrite the value of their small-brand portfolios — worth tracking now for any partner whose Ohio contracts renew automatically.

Around the States

New York moves delinquent cannabis retailers to cash-on-delivery as unpaid supplier bills reach $3.9 million

NEW YORK • MJBizDaily • Sep 17

About 87 licensed retailers — roughly 12% of the state's outlets — owe 44 suppliers a combined $3.9 million, an average of about $45,000 per store and 281 days past New York's 30-day payment deadline. The Office of Cannabis Management tracks the violations through the Metrc system and places delinquent accounts on an official cash-on-delivery list requiring upfront payment; officials reviewed the data at a September 10 Cannabis Advisory Board meeting.

Why it matters: Anyone extending trade credit into New York's THC retail channel should check counterparties against the COD list — and note the unresolved industry question of excise tax owed on invoiced sales the retailer has not yet paid.

Missouri recalls infused pre-rolls after aspergillus test failure, its second recall this month

MISSOURI • Missouri Independent • Sep 18

The Division of Cannabis Regulation recalled Juicy J's cherry limeade infused pre-rolls Friday after state testing found aspergillus mold, and said it is working with the Waynesville manufacturing facility to address the problem. No adverse reactions had been reported; it is the agency's second cannabis recall in September.

On the Radar

Sep 22 — Alaska's Marijuana Control Board holds a special meeting on its Administrative Order 360 regulatory overhaul, considering removal of the daily concentrate and total-THC purchase limits plus changes to packaging, transportation, tracking and labeling rules; any changes would still go through formal rulemaking (state public notice).

Sep 24 — Michigan Investments 10, maker of the Muha Meds line, must cease operations under the license revocation and record $2 million fine covered in yesterday's edition; Michigan partners should complete inventory decisions before that date (MJBizDaily).

Sep 29 — The executive order barring imports of Canadian beer, wine and spirits takes effect; product entered for consumption before that date remains subject to the existing 50% duty instead (Shanken News Daily).

Dec 11 — The federal ban on consumable hemp products above 0.4 mg total THC per container takes effect; synthetic cannabinoids not naturally producible from the plant are recriminalized on the original Nov 12 date (Marijuana Moment).
