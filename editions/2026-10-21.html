Top Story

Delaware’s THC beverage law takes effect today, opening sales at package stores and dispensaries

DELAWARE • Delaware General Assembly • Oct 21

House Bill 373, signed July 23, takes effect today and sets Delaware’s first full framework for making, distributing and selling THC-infused beverages. The law names each tier that must hold an authorization: manufacturers (in-state and out-of-state) must be authorized to operate and must deliver product to licensed importers, who must meet notice, testing and shipment-recordkeeping requirements before beverages move to retail; retail sales run through package stores authorized for off-premises sales and licensed retail marijuana stores. The act sets a $0.50-per-container tax collected at the warehouse-distributor level (not at retail) and raises civil penalties for under-21 sales of infused beverages to the greater of $250 or up to 10% of average gross monthly sales; it does not set new license fee amounts on its face. Sales end automatically if federal law reclassifies hemp as a controlled substance. The governor’s signing was reported by Marijuana Moment in July.

Why it matters: A regulated Delaware channel for THC beverages opens today, but market access runs through licensed importers, and the per-container tax is collected at the distributor tier — partners selling into Delaware need importer arrangements, testing documentation and tax handling in place before shipping.

Federal

THC drink maker sends six-packs to members of Congress as federal limits approach

FEDERAL • Marijuana Moment • Oct 19

Herb Light, a maker of low-dose hemp THC drinks, launched a “Cheers Against Prohibition” campaign that lets customers buying a 12-pack add a six-pack delivered to their members of Congress with a letter urging support for pending legislation that would keep hemp THC beverages federally legal under a regulatory system. Co-founder Gordy Sang said “most consumers don’t even know there’s a federal ban in the works.” Under current law, products above 0.4 mg total THC per container lose federal hemp status on December 11.

Around the States

Colorado audit finds gaps in marijuana product-testing oversight

COLORADO • Marijuana Moment / Colorado Newsline • Oct 20

A state audit presented to the Legislative Audit Committee found that the Marijuana Enforcement Division lets businesses select their own test samples (auditors said 24 other legal states use a third party), inspects businesses on a two-year goal cycle, and from 2023–2025 averaged more than seven months before issuing advisories on potentially harmful products. Auditors said they found no evidence of widespread health and safety problems; MED said it agreed with all of the audit’s recommendations.

Why it matters: Auditors recommended strengthening the state reference lab and lawmakers could take up third-party sampling next session — a change that would add testing logistics and cost for Colorado suppliers and the retailers they stock.

Two polls show Massachusetts voters opposed to Question 8 repeal of legal sales

MASSACHUSETTS • Marijuana Moment • Oct 20

Question 8 on the November 3 ballot would repeal the laws allowing regulated commercial cannabis sales and home cultivation while keeping possession and the medical program. A UMass Amherst/WCVB poll found 61% of registered voters would vote no and 31% yes; a Suffolk University/Boston Globe poll found 71% opposed and 21% in support.

Why it matters: A yes vote would close the state’s commercial sales channel; retailers and distributors with Massachusetts exposure have a decision point on November 3.

On the Radar

Nov. 12 / Dec. 11 — The federal ban on synthetic cannabinoids in hemp products takes effect November 12; the 0.3% total-THC standard and 0.4 mg per-container cap for finished products follow December 11 after the September continuing resolution split the timeline, per a Vicente LLP client alert. Bills that would create a regulatory carve-out for hemp beverages, including the Beverage Regulatory Parity Act, remain in committee.

Nov. 13 — New Jersey’s transition period for hemp beverages ends November 13; legislation is pending that would extend it to the federal compliance date, per the same Vicente LLP alert.

Nov. 3 — Massachusetts votes on Question 8, which would repeal regulated commercial cannabis sales.
