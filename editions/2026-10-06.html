Top Story

Delta-8's federal cutoff date remains unclear as FDA misses guidance deadline

FEDERAL • MJBizDaily • Oct 5

Under the federal hemp law enacted last winter, products over 0.4 milligrams of total THC per container become controlled substances on December 11 — but delta-8 and other converted cannabinoids may fall under an earlier November 12 date that applies to lab-synthesized cannabinoids, and the FDA has not published the guidance that would settle which date governs. Jonathan Miller of the U.S. Hemp Roundtable said the group is "very frustrated with the FDA's failure to meet this statutory deadline." Operators including Texas-based Hometown Hero said they are liquidating delta-8 inventory and reformulating product lines state by state.

Why it matters: Retailers and distributors still holding delta-8 or other converted-cannabinoid products cannot safely assume the December 11 date applies — sell-down plans keyed to November 12 are the conservative path until FDA publishes its guidance.

Federal

Treasury and IRS put cannabis tax guidance on the 2026–27 priority plan

FEDERAL • Marijuana Moment • Oct 5

The agencies' new Priority Guidance Plan for the fiscal year that began October 1 lists guidance under Section 280E among 121 priority projects. After the April move of FDA-approved products and state-licensed medical cannabis to Schedule III, 280E's bar on ordinary business deductions no longer reaches those operations, and the coming guidance is expected to address how businesses serving both medical and adult-use markets apportion it. No completion deadline is set.

Why it matters: Retail partners with cannabis-side operations get their first formal signal on how deduction eligibility will be split across medical and adult-use revenue — a direct cash-tax question for 2026 returns.

DEA's updated Drugs of Abuse report reflects post-rescheduling landscape

FEDERAL • Marijuana Moment • Oct 5

The agency's 2026 edition of its 119-page reference report states that no deaths from marijuana overdose have been reported and, in a change from the 2024 edition, acknowledges that most U.S. states have legalized medical sales. The report also notes impairment risks and increased emergency-room visits involving edibles; a broader rescheduling proceeding covering additional cannabis products remains pending.

Around the States

Rhode Island hemp-THC sellers wind down ahead of December 11 federal limit

RHODE ISLAND • Rhode Island Current • Oct 6

The state Cannabis Control Commission, which issued a September 11 bulletin warning of fines for noncompliance, told businesses the 0.4-milligram federal cap will override Rhode Island's current 1-milligram-per-serving rules; chief legal counsel Mariana Ormonde said "federal law is the supreme law of the land." Beverages account for $2.36 million of the state's $2.85 million in 2025 hemp product sales across 201 licensed hemp businesses, and growers said they cannot plan 2027 planting until the rules settle.

Why it matters: A preview of the sell-down now facing hemp beverage retailers in every state — compliant-today inventory above 0.4 mg total THC per container has no legal channel after December 11.

Massachusetts governor says she will vote against November repeal measure

MASSACHUSETTS • Marijuana Moment • Oct 5

The November ballot measure would repeal licensed recreational sales and home cultivation while keeping possession legal and the medical program intact. Gov. Maura Healey (D) said "I'd vote no. We've got to keep recreational marijuana here in Massachusetts," and Cannabis Control Commission chair Chris Harding said repeal "could eliminate thousands of jobs, hundreds of businesses and billions in tax revenue." Recent polls show opposition to the measure between 55 and 71 percent, with support between 24 and 33 percent.

On the Radar

Nov. 12 / Dec. 11 — The federal hemp redefinition's two effective dates: synthesized cannabinoids may become controlled November 12 under some readings, and the 0.4 mg total-THC-per-container limit takes effect December 11. FDA guidance that would resolve the split is overdue.

Oct. 21 — Oregon regulators are scheduled to consider changes to hemp product registration rules as the state aligns with the federal timeline.

Late October — The Virginia Cannabis Control Authority's preliminary-feedback window on its draft adult-use retail rules approaches, with final rules expected in December and license applications opening February 1, 2027.
