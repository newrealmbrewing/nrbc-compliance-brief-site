TOP STORY

Democrats take both chambers of Congress, putting the federal cannabis and hemp agenda in new hands

FEDERAL • Marijuana Moment • Nov 4

Democrats won majorities in both the U.S. House and Senate in Tuesday's midterms, with a projected Senate margin of 54–56 seats as of Wednesday. Expected House Speaker Hakeem Jeffries and Senate Majority Leader Chuck Schumer have each sponsored bills to remove cannabis from the Controlled Substances Act, though the Senate's 60-vote filibuster threshold has blocked past reforms, including banking access. NORML political director Morgan Fox said two-thirds of Americans support ending prohibition and urged Congress to act, while the Drug Policy Alliance's Cat Packer called for a federal framework covering both marijuana and hemp; the Trump administration, for its part, has pursued rescheduling rather than legalization, moving medical cannabis to Schedule III earlier this year.

Why it matters: The new Congress controls whether the December 11 federal hemp-THC restrictions stand, slip again, or are rewritten — the biggest open variable in THC-beverage inventory, ordering, and shelf-planning decisions for distributors and retailers this quarter.

FEDERAL

Senator-elect Talarico says he will work with the White House on federal cannabis legalization

FEDERAL • Marijuana Moment • Nov 5

Sen.-elect James Talarico (D-TX) said he plans to work with President Trump on federal legalization and wants records expunged for people convicted of marijuana offenses. His comments add a newly elected voice to the debate over how far the incoming Congress will go beyond the administration's rescheduling track.

AROUND THE STATES

Pennsylvania poised to legalize marijuana as Democrats claim state Senate majority

PENNSYLVANIA • Marijuana Moment • Nov 4

Democrats appear to have flipped the Pennsylvania Senate for the first time in over three decades while keeping the House, and reelected Gov. Josh Shapiro (D) said he expects a trifecta. Pending legislation (SB 120) would allow adults 21+ to possess cannabis and would layer an 8% excise tax on top of the 6% sales tax; the state's Independent Fiscal Office projects nearly half a billion dollars in annual revenue by 2028. Senate Majority Leader Joe Pittman (R) said he does not expect legalization this year and that the state should wait for further federal changes.

Why it matters: A legal adult-use market in a top-ten-population state would reshape beverage shelf competition across the Mid-Atlantic — and the House-passed model routes sales through state-run dispensaries, so watch which retail structure survives negotiation.

Michigan opens full e-filing for the new wholesale marijuana tax on November 12

MICHIGAN • Michigan Dept. of Treasury • Nov 4

Michigan Treasury will open registration, filing, and payment for the wholesale marijuana tax in its eServices portal at 7:00 a.m. on November 12; the tax is unavailable in eServices November 4–11, so no WMT payments can be submitted during the blackout. Taxpayers must register the CRA license number for each liable location — using the numbers that made quarterly payments for the first three quarters of 2026 — and all 2026 quarterly returns are due January 20, 2027.

Why it matters: This is a per-location registration obligation for every licensed wholesaler in the state — partners handling Michigan product should calendar the November 12 opening and the January 20 return deadline now, and plan payments around this week's blackout.

Statehouse shifts put legalization on the table in Wisconsin and Iowa

WISCONSIN / IOWA • Marijuana Moment • Nov 5

Wisconsin Democrats gained control of the state legislature alongside a newly elected governor who supports legalization, a combination that could put medical or recreational cannabis in reach for the first time. In Iowa, Gov.-elect Rob Sand (D) said he wants to legalize marijuana to help address state budget issues, arguing reform would also simplify law enforcement's job.

ON THE RADAR

Nov. 12 — Illinois's reclassification of hemp-derived THC drinks takes effect, ending sales through conventional retail, and Michigan's wholesale marijuana tax e-filing opens the same day.

Dec. 7 — Comments are due to TTB on the renewal of 16 information collections — forms and recordkeeping requirements that touch permittees across all three tiers.

Dec. 11 — The federal hemp-THC restrictions take effect after September's one-month delay, excluding products over 0.4 mg total THC per container from the federal hemp definition — whether the new Congress revisits the date is now the question to watch.
