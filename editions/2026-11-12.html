TOP STORY

Illinois hemp THC restrictions take effect today; most sales move to dispensaries only

ILLINOIS • Illinois Dept. of Revenue • Nov 12

Illinois's hemp law takes effect today, November 12. Intoxicating hemp products — including beverages above 0.4 milligrams of total THC per container — are now classified under the state's cannabis framework, and their sale is restricted to licensed dispensaries; the Department of Revenue's bulletin FY 2027-04 confirms the effective date and the tax reclassification that follows it. Enforcement remains paused through November 19 for the roughly 250 businesses in the Illinois Healthy Alternatives Association's federal lawsuit, and a federal judge hears the group's motions to block the law that same day.

Why it matters: Illinois retail and distribution partners outside the lawsuit group must have hemp THC beverages above the 0.4 mg threshold off shelves as of today; dispensaries are the only lawful channel unless the November 19 hearing broadens relief.

FEDERAL

DEA rejects medical cannabis business registrations in at least six states and D.C.

FEDERAL • Marijuana Moment • Nov 11

The DEA has sent orders to show cause rejecting medical marijuana businesses seeking federal registration and rescheduling protections in California, Delaware, Illinois, North Dakota, Oregon and Washington, D.C., after approving more than 900 applicants in seven states last month. The orders say certain state rules — such as allowing access without a physician's written certification — conflict with the Controlled Substances Act's closed medical system and treaty obligations.

Why it matters: which suppliers hold federal registration will shape Schedule III protections across state supply chains, and DEA guidance requires registration seekers to source only from registered or timely-applied companies by late November.

Senate appropriators encourage hemp cultivar development, raise cannabinoid-food concerns

FEDERAL • Marijuana Moment • Nov 12

Senate Appropriations Committee spending reports encouraged hemp cultivar and germplasm development, raised concerns about cannabinoid-infused foods, and directed agencies to consider medical marijuana as an opioid alternative. Separately, the USDA asked the White House budget office to extend a study of hemp production practices and costs.

AROUND THE STATES

Minnesota businesses scale back hemp THC lines ahead of December 11 federal ban

MINNESOTA • FOX 9 • Nov 11

With the federal ban on hemp-derived THC drinks and gummies set for December 11, Minnesota operators are diverging: MinnyGrown has stopped making hemp products, Total Wine has let its THC shelves go empty, Top Ten Liquors is expanding the category, and Indeed Brewing's Ryan Bandy said payment-processing barriers are already cutting into sales. The state recorded $180 million in hemp THC retail sales in 2025.

Why it matters: in the country's most developed THC-beverage retail market, mainstream shelf capacity is contracting a month before the federal deadline — partners holding inventory need sell-through or return plans now.

Maryland now allows county-by-county wholesaler appointments for RTDs

MARYLAND • ArentFox Schiff • Nov 9

Under revised rules effective November 9, suppliers of “non-conforming beverages” — single-serve, sealed, non-resealable containers at or under 20% ABV containing wine or spirits plus a non-alcoholic component — may appoint a different wholesaler in each Maryland county instead of one statewide. Territories must be exclusive and county-wide, wholesaler changes require 30 days' notice to the affected wholesalers and the Alcohol, Tobacco, and Cannabis Commission, and master/sub-distributor “daisy chains” are no longer permitted for these products.

Why it matters: suppliers can now align RTD distribution with county-level beer networks; existing statewide appointments stand unless changed, and distributors should expect appointment notices as suppliers re-map territories.

ON THE RADAR

Nov. 19 — U.S. District Court hearing in Chicago on the Illinois hemp industry's restraining-order and preliminary-injunction motions; the state's enforcement pause for the ~250 lawsuit businesses expires the same day (CBS Chicago).

Late November — DEA deadline for businesses seeking federal registration to source cannabis only from companies that have been granted or have timely applied for registration (Marijuana Moment).

Dec. 11 — the federal hemp definition change takes effect; hemp-derived THC drinks and gummies above the new thresholds lose federal hemp status nationwide (FOX 9).
