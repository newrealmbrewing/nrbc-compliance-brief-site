Top Story

Texas judge hears bid to pause THC enforcement; ruling could come within days

TEXAS • FOX 7 Austin • Aug 28

A Travis County judge on Friday heard arguments from hemp businesses seeking a temporary restraining order against the Texas Department of State Health Services' “Total THC” rules, which took effect July 31 and impose a 0.3% total-THC limit on consumable hemp products while raising annual license fees from about $250 to $10,000. Attorney David Sergi, representing the businesses, argued the Legislature never defined “trace amount,” leaving the standard to regulators; Aubree Adams of Citizens for a Safe and Healthy Texas said, “Texas can hold a line and say, ‘No, you're not allowed in our state.’” The court could rule at any time; if the TRO is granted, a temporary-injunction hearing would follow on Sept. 17 or Oct. 8.

Why it matters: The ruling will determine whether hemp THC products, including beverages, can lawfully be sold in Texas while the case proceeds — a swing factor for retail and distribution partners deciding on Texas inventory this fall.

Federal

Bill to delay federal hemp restrictions to 2028 reaches 41 House backers

FEDERAL • The Marijuana Herald • Aug 28

Reps. Rob Wittman (R-VA) and Gil Cisneros (D-CA) signed on Aug. 27 to H.R. 7024, the Hemp Planting Predictability Act, bringing support to 41 members across both parties. Sponsored by Rep. James Baird (R-IN), the bill would move the effective date of the new federal hemp restrictions — including the total-THC standard and derived-cannabinoid limits — from Nov. 12, 2026 to Nov. 12, 2028; it awaits action in the House Agriculture Committee, with companion S. 3686 pending in the Senate.

Why it matters: Absent a delay, the Nov. 12 changes would make most hemp-derived THC beverages federally unlawful — the largest compliance date on the industry calendar.

Around the States

New Mexico businesses press for emergency order as tracking-system shutdown nears

NEW MEXICO • The Marijuana Herald • Aug 29

Seven cannabis businesses, backed by the Cannabis Association of New Mexico, are seeking a temporary restraining order in the Second Judicial District Court to keep the BioTrack system running while the state's new Seed-to-Sale System is tested; the state filed a motion to dismiss on Aug. 24, and no ruling had issued as of Friday. Testing labs stop receiving samples Aug. 31, retailers switch over Sept. 2, and BioTrack shuts down Sept. 4.

On the Radar

Sept. 8 — Canadian retaliatory measures on U.S. alcohol take effect, including Saskatchewan's 50% levy on U.S.-made products — a date exporters and their distributor partners are watching for Canadian order flow.

Nov. 12, 2026 — The federal hemp redefinition takes effect absent congressional action: a 0.3% total-THC standard and a 0.4 mg total-THC-per-container cap on finished products. FDA implementation guidance remains pending.

DEA rescheduling — The administrative judge's recommendation on moving marijuana to Schedule III remains pending following the Aug. 26 release of the hearing transcript.
