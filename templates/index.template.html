    About

    The NRBC Compliance Brief is a daily morning email from New Realm Brewing covering regulatory and compliance developments in alcohol and hemp/THC beverages, written for New Realm’s leadership team and its distributor and retail partners — and open to anyone in the industry. It reports what happened — agency actions, votes, effective dates, court rulings, enforcement — with every position attributed to its named actor and every headline linked to the original source. To join the list, use the Subscribe button above or email jbarnes@newrealmbrewing.com with the subject “Subscribe - Compliance Brief.” Unsubscribe anytime from any edition.

    Jeremy Barnes | Chief Financial Officer, New Realm Brewing Co.
