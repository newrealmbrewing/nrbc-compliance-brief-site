Virginia Adult-Use Cannabis Rollout: Rules, Fees & Key Dates

The one-page status of Virginia's adult-use retail market: the draft rules, the fee schedule, the application calendar, and every Virginia development covered by the daily brief — including the state's separate 2 mg hemp-THC cap.

Last updated Wednesday, October 7, 2026 • Maintained daily by the NRBC Compliance Brief

Where Things Stand

On September 9, 2026 the Virginia Cannabis Control Authority released an 82-page draft of the regulations that will govern the state's adult-use retail market — license types and caps, fees, delivery, purchase limits and store siting. State law caps the market at 350 retail stores and five Tier V cultivation facilities. "These are not final by any means," said Jake Shuford, the CCA's legislative and regulatory manager; the authority expects preliminary stakeholder feedback by late October and plans to finalize the rules in December 2026. The Compliance Brief's special edition on the draft rules covers the full fee schedule and operating rules.

Timeline

  • August 14, 2026 — In effect

    Virginia's 2 mg hemp-THC cap takes effect (separate track)

    HB 30 bans hemp products above 2 mg total THC per package and ends the 25:1 CBD-to-THC ratio exception, after a federal judge declined to block it — the hemp-side backdrop to the adult-use rollout.

  • September 9, 2026 — Published

    CCA releases draft adult-use retail regulations

    82 pages covering license types and caps, the fee schedule below, home delivery (authorized) versus drive-thru and curbside (prohibited), 2-ounce transaction limits, and a 1,000-foot setback from schools and hospitals. Where applications exceed available licenses, the CCA anticipates lottery selection.

  • Late October 2026 — Open now

    Preliminary stakeholder feedback window

    The CCA's Regulations Input page is collecting comments, with preliminary feedback expected by late October — the window to shape the rules before they harden.

  • December 2026 — Expected

    Final regulations adopted

  • February 1, 2027 — Applications open

    First application phase begins

    Microbusinesses, hemp processors, cultivators and testing labs apply first; retail applications follow. Initial licenses are due to be issued by May 1, 2027.

  • July 1, 2027 — Launch

    Retail sales begin

    Licensees then have 18 months from licensure to become operational or forfeit the license.

Opening one Virginia retail store under the draft costs $24,000 up front ($4,000 application + $20,000 authorization) plus $15,000 a year thereafter, per location — with only 350 store licenses statewide.
