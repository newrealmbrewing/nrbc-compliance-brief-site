Top Story

North Carolina panel floats state-run cannabis stores, private cultivation licenses

NORTH CAROLINA • Marijuana Moment • Sep 21

The Market and Regulatory Subcommittee of Gov. Josh Stein’s (D) Advisory Council on Cannabis discussed a framework that would place retail cannabis sales exclusively in state-controlled stores — drawing on North Carolina’s ABC liquor-retail model and Quebec’s — while reserving cultivation and manufacturing for private businesses under multiple license types (craft farm, commercial cultivation, processor). Three wholesale structures are on the table: private licensed distributors, a state-operated central warehouse, or a hybrid. NC ABC’s Renee Metz presented at the meeting; the council’s final report to the legislature is due December 31, 2026, and no fee schedule has been proposed yet.

Why it matters: As drafted, private retailers would have no path to a cannabis retail license in North Carolina, and private distributors’ access to the wholesale tier depends on which of the three distribution options the council recommends by December 31 — worth watching closely for any partner planning a North Carolina cannabis or hemp adjacency.

Federal

Fourth Circuit panel questions DEA’s Schedule I designation of hemp-derived HHC

FEDERAL • Law360 • Sep 18

At oral argument Friday, a Fourth Circuit panel that included Chief Judge Albert Diaz and Judge Toby Heytens appeared skeptical of the rationale the DEA used to designate hexahydrocannabinol (HHC), a psychoactive compound synthesized from hemp extracts, as a Schedule I substance. Hemp companies challenging the rule argue HHC qualifies as a legal hemp derivative under the 2018 Farm Bill; the case was flagged in Marijuana Moment’s Monday roundup.

Why it matters: The decision will help define how far DEA can go in scheduling hemp-derived cannabinoids by administrative action — the same authority question that hangs over other converted cannabinoids used in hemp beverages.

California cannabis chief: DEA has issued no rescheduling implementation guidance to states

FEDERAL / CALIFORNIA • Marijuana Moment • Sep 21

California Department of Cannabis Control Director Clint Kellum told the state’s Cannabis Advisory Committee that regulators “have received no formal updates from DEA headquarters on implementation guidance” since the attorney general moved state-regulated medical marijuana to Schedule III in April — even as DEA conducts on-site inspections of registered businesses and requests state program data. State regulators say the gap leaves them unable to advise licensees on federal compliance steps.

Around the States

Colorado sets Q4 average market rates behind its 15% marijuana excise tax

COLORADO • Colo. Dept. of Revenue • Sep 22

The Department of Revenue published new average market rates effective October 1–December 31: retail bud $601 per pound, trim $204, bud allocated for extraction $425, trim for extraction $100, immature plants $18 each. The AMR is the valuation basis for the 15% excise tax on transfers between cultivators and affiliated stores or manufacturers, recalculated quarterly; a separate hearing on proposed retail marijuana tax rules is set for October 15.

Alabama health board withdraws objection to marijuana rescheduling under state law

ALABAMA • Marijuana Moment • Sep 19

The Alabama Department of Public Health’s governing body voted unanimously on September 18 to withdraw the objection it filed in May to aligning state scheduling with the federal move of state-regulated medical marijuana to Schedule III. State Health Officer Dr. Scott Harris said the department’s legal team had resolved its implementation concerns for pharmacies and patients.

On the Radar

September 29 — The presidential order excluding Canadian wine, spirits and beer from U.S. importation takes effect; product imported earlier but not yet entered for consumption stays subject to the existing 50% duty (Shanken News Daily).

December 11 — The federal hemp redefinition limiting legal products to 0.4 mg total THC per container takes effect, after Congress moved the date back from November 12 in the funding bill signed September 4 (Marijuana Moment).

October 15 — Colorado’s Department of Revenue holds its hearing on proposed retail marijuana tax rule changes; written comment is open until the hearing (notice).
